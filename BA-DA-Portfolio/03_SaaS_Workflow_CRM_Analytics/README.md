# SaaS Workflow Automation / CRM Analytics

## Business Problem
A B2B SaaS company wants to understand which customers, segments and products drive revenue and profit, where discounts hurt margins, and how the sales pipeline can be made more efficient.

## Dataset
AWS SaaS Sales dataset (Kaggle). Search 'AWS SaaS Sales' on kaggle.com.
(Download from Kaggle into `data/`. Raw data is not committed.)

## Business Questions
1. What are monthly revenue, profit and order trends?
2. Which customer segments and industries generate the most profit?
3. Which products are high revenue but low (or negative) profit?
4. How do discounts affect profit margin?
5. Which regions/countries are growing or declining?
6. Which customers are top accounts, and who is at risk (declining orders)?
